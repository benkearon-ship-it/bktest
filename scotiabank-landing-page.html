Current Deployment Challenges

Common pain points facing modern banking software delivery

⚠️

Disjointed Release Management

Manual processes and lack of stability force releases to occur at night due to risk, impacting team velocity and reliability.

🔍

Manual Verification Processes

Time-consuming verification across multiple sources with manual correlation communicated via email, consuming significant resources.

🔗

Complex Dependencies

Many steps and dependencies in delivery process with lack of guardrails, leading to high developer toil and context switching.

🚨

High Incident Occurrence

HOT/HOT deployments are challenging, canary rollouts require custom scripting, and no mechanism exists for automated rollback.

🔧

Limited Flexibility

Solutions tailor-made for specific paradigms (GKE, Anthos) lack flexibility to pivot. No common solution across legacy, k8s, and serverless.

⏱️

Pipeline Maintenance Burden

Teams expend majority of resources maintaining pipelines, detracting from time that could be spent innovating or modernizing.
